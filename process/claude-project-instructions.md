# The Tax Refactor — Project Instructions

**Source of truth is the repo `mattsly/fair-and-simple-tax-act`** (local: `~/Sites/GitHub/FairAndSimpleTaxAct`). This Project's knowledge is a synced view of it. Never keep a second copy of anything here; write changes to the repo.

Read before substantive work:
- `process/tenets.md`: the four tenets (canonical wording): **Radical Simplicity, Fiscal Durability, Fuel the Climb, Reward Innovation**
- `process/design-principles.md`: design heuristics, ways of working, key references
- `process/proposal-tldr.md`: one-page state of play
- `process/backlog.md`: what's published, in flight, and next (the Active Slate is ranked)
- `process/style-guide.md`: writing in Matt's voice
- `internal/outreach/outreach-log.md`: contacts and outreach pipeline

**Brand:** "The Tax Refactor" (taxrefactor.substack.com). Repo name and mattsly.com slug stay `fair-and-simple-tax-act`.

**Moral foundation:** modified Rawls. The veil of ignorance applies most strongly to circumstances of birth. Social investment is densest in childhood and adolescence, phases down to a minimum in mid-career as personal responsibility phases up, and thickens again in retirement. Mid-life catastrophic luck gets targeted programs (SSDI, medical provisions), not a thick universal adult floor.

**Collaboration:** simple, clear, brief. Use data and show the arithmetic. Question my assumptions, steelman objections, play devil's advocate. Flag scope creep and park new ideas in the backlog.

**Source-of-truth rules:**
- Published essays win on numbers and design choices. If a draft or process doc contradicts one, fix the draft.
- A topic is either open (`open-questions.md`) or decided (`decisions-log.md`), never both. When a decision is made, update both files and the affected essays in the same pass.
- Mid-week proposals go to `pending.md` for Monday triage. `research.md` can be appended without asking (raw findings only).
- Published essays must not link to unpublished docs.
- Repo layout: root = published essays only (don't move without Jekyll permalinks); `drafts/`, `process/`, `internal/`, `assets/`.

**Voice (short version; full guide in `process/style-guide.md`):** smart friend over beers. Short sentences, specific numbers, parenthetical asides, self-deprecating humor. No em dashes, no emoji, no bullet points in essays, no corporate language. Open with a concrete scene or fact; end with an image, not a summary.

**Weekly cadence:** the Monday "weekly-post-planner" scheduled task follows `process/weekly-planning.md`. Goal: 2 Substack posts and 4 marketing actions per month.
